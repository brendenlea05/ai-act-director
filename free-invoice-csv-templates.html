How to use the templates

  1. Fill each CSV with exports from your own accounting, inventory or purchasing records, using consistent PO and SKU identifiers. Keep invoice IDs unique where appropriate.
  2. For goods receipts, enter the quantity actually accepted, not just the ordered quantity. Confirm whether partial deliveries and returns are represented.
  3. Open the free LineVerity checker, choose your three CSVs, confirm column mappings and run the audit.
  4. Review flags for quantity or unit-price differences, then download a CSV discrepancy report or vendor-specific draft packet.
  5. Check every flag against original agreements, credits, taxes, freight and receiving history before contacting a supplier or making a payment decision.
