Billed versus received quantity: identify mismatches

A quantity mismatch occurs when a supplier bills for more units than your goods-receipt records show as accepted. It may indicate an early invoice, missing receipt entry or genuine discrepancy.

What this check means

A quantity mismatch occurs when a supplier bills for more units than your goods-receipt records show as accepted. It may indicate an early invoice, missing receipt entry or genuine discrepancy.

What records you need

Keep purchase order, receiving and invoice data for the same period. Use consistent SKUs and units; if receiving reports show cartons while the invoice shows eaches, convert units before matching. Aggregate receipt and invoice quantities by PO and SKU, then compare totals.

What to investigate

An invoice might cover goods in transit, a partial receipt may be posted after the export or backorders may be billed separately. Review timing and receiving cutoffs before disputing any amount.
