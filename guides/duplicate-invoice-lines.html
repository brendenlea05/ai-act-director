Accounts payable / duplicate lines

When two invoice lines look identical, what should you check?

Two identical rows in an invoice export can be a duplicated import, an accidental double charge, or a perfectly valid entry for two separate lots. The right response is a quick document check, not an automatic payment block.

A simple duplicate-line example

An invoice export shows invoice INV-090, PO-1041, SKU A, quantity 5 and unit price $40. The next row shows the exact same five values. That is a possible duplicate worth $200 on the second row. Before treating it as a charge error, look at the vendor's source invoice and any lot, shipment or line-sequence fields not included in the export.

Questions to resolve before escalating

  1. Did the export repeat a row? Check whether the same source record was included in two report pulls.
  2. Were there two shipments? Split deliveries may be represented as identical invoice rows.
  3. Do the documents agree? Compare the actual vendor invoice and the accepted receiving records.
  4. Was a credit already issued? Look for returns, corrective invoices and credit memos.
  5. Has either line been paid? Duplicate invoice lines are not the same as duplicate payments.

What LineVerity looks for

The free checker flags repeated invoice-number, PO, SKU, quantity and unit-price combinations within the uploaded data. It also compares total billed units to ordered and received units. It does not search historical payments or investigate whether separate invoice IDs represent the same underlying goods.

When the records remain unclear, a short note to the supplier citing the invoice number and exact rows is often the useful next step.

Review possible duplicates in CSVs

Possible duplicates require human verification. Do not automatically stop payments based on matching text fields alone. No supplier messages are sent by the checker.

Frequently asked question

Can I automatically block invoices based on this result?

No. Investigate documentation, credits, shipment timing and payment records before taking any financial action.
