Duplicate invoice lines: detect possible double billing

Duplicate invoice lines can result in accidental double counting when the same invoice line is imported or submitted twice. Identical rows deserve review, but are not automatically fraudulent or incorrect.

What this check means

Duplicate invoice lines can result in accidental double counting when the same invoice line is imported or submitted twice. Identical rows deserve review, but are not automatically fraudulent or incorrect.

What records you need

Compare invoice identifier, purchase order, SKU, unit price and quantity. Two identical rows under one invoice identifier are a possible duplicate. Also compare the sum of all billed quantities with the ordered and accepted quantities for the same PO/SKU.

What to investigate

Suppliers may intentionally split lines across batches, dates or lots. In some systems a duplicated line is correct for two distinct shipments, while different invoice IDs can also repeat the same charge. This free checker is not a full historical duplicate-payments detector.
