Privacy and data handling

Last updated October 28, 2026

Your CSVs stay on your device

LineVerity reads the purchase-order, receipt, and invoice CSV files you select using JavaScript in your browser. The site's application code does not send those files to a LineVerity server, call an AI API, set tracking cookies, create a user account, or store your input in a database. Generated reports are downloaded to your device.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider may automatically process ordinary website request data, such as IP addresses, browsers, timestamps, and paths. That is separate from the local CSV calculations. We do not currently run our own marketing analytics or advertising pixels.

Email inquiries

If you voluntarily email brendenlea05ai@gmail.com, your email and any information you provide will be handled using Gmail. Please do not send confidential invoices, customer details, banking records, or sensitive business data by email.

Optional digital kit purchases

The free checker does not collect payment information. If you purchase the separate Supplier Recovery Action Kit, Stripe hosts the checkout and handles card and billing information under its own terms. Our connected Stripe merchant account records the order amount, payment status, checkout reference and the email you enter for delivery; we don't receive or store your full card number. The payment merchant on a statement may appear as GROVIABID AI. We use the purchaser email with Gmail to send the digital ZIP and to provide order-related help, and retain records for order support, fraud prevention and legal obligations. We do not add you to promotional mailing lists unless you opt in separately.

Questions

For privacy questions, email brendenlea05ai@gmail.com.
