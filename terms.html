Free beta terms and limitations

Last updated October 27, 2026

Purpose

LineVerity is a free, experimental checking tool intended to assist people who work with purchasing and inventory spreadsheets. It compares lines using deterministic rules. It is not an accounting system, legal service, payment blocker, audit certification, or substitute for professional judgment.

Possible discrepancies, not confirmed loss

Results are potential discrepancies for investigation. They may reflect partial deliveries, split invoice lines, credits, taxes, units, currency issues, legitimate contract exceptions, or incomplete exports. Confirm every finding against source records, agreements, and business procedures before contacting vendors or making payment decisions. No financial recovery is promised.

Data and permitted use

Only upload CSV files you are permitted to access and process. The app runs in your browser. Do not rely on it for time-sensitive or irreversible payment decisions without independent review. Features may change during beta testing and the site may be unavailable.

Availability and warranties

This beta is provided on an as-available basis without a service-level guarantee. We make no representations about completeness, accuracy, availability, or suitability for a specific accounting or purchasing workflow. Applicable consumer and other legal rights are not excluded.

Pricing

The checker is currently free. There is no checkout, subscription, or paid upgrade connected to this website. Any future paid product will identify its own terms before purchase.
