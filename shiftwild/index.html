A few things worth knowing

THE DETAILS.

Is this a printed poster?

Not yet. Your $19.95 purchase is one personalized high-resolution digital JPG delivered by email, not a shipped print or framed poster. You can print it personally if you wish.

Can I actually change my car's background?

You can change color treatments instantly or upload your own background image. You can put a transparent vehicle cutout over a new scene, or manually erase the old photo background using the studio brush. The brush is a simple manual tool, not automatic AI background removal; intricate edges may require more time. Send us both your original image and the design description.

Where does my uploaded photo go?

Your image stays inside your browser while you're editing. We do not upload the photo from the studio. To place an order, you must email the photo to us separately. Please send only images you own or have permission to use.

When will my cover arrive?

Within two business days after you have paid and emailed a suitable image and design details. If we need another photograph or clarification, we'll email you. Business days exclude weekends and U.S. federal holidays.

Can I request changes or a refund?

One minor text correction is included if requested within seven days of delivery. Contact us if the design isn't what you ordered. Customized digital orders are reviewed individually, and applicable consumer rights remain unaffected. If we cannot fulfill your order, we will refund it.

Are you affiliated with any vehicle brands?

No. SHIFTWILD is an independent editorial artwork concept and is not sponsored by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any automobile manufacturer. Vehicle names may be used descriptively. Brand identity is provisional pending clearance.
